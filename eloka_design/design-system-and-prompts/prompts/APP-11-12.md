Two onboarding steps that use the standard step layout: the documents checklist (step 9) and the bank account (step 10).

**PLATFORM:** Web, Mobile-first

**FRAME A — Documents:**
1. Title "Business documents", with a helper line: "Private Limited company: we need these 2 documents. JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 10 MB each."
2. A "Required" group of document tiles:
   - "Address proof": an uploaded file "address-proof.pdf · 1.2 MB", status pill "Waiting for review", actions View / Replace / Remove.
   - "Certificate of incorporation": not uploaded yet, shown as an upload drop area "Tap to upload or drag a file here".
3. An "Optional" group: tiles for "GST certificate", "Board resolution" and "Other". (No "PAN card" tile: the PAN is already verified in step 4.)
4. Upload-in-progress variant: one tile shows a progress bar at 60% and a "Cancel" link.
5. Rejected variant: one tile shows the pill "Rejected" and a reviewer-note strip "The stamp is not readable — upload a clearer scan", with a "Replace" button.
6. A validation message variant at the top: "Upload these to continue: Certificate of incorporation".
7. Sticky primary button "Continue".

**Desktop version of Frame A:** the step rail on the left; documents laid out in a single column at 720px.

**FRAME B — Bank account:**
1. Title "Settlement bank account".
2. Fields, each with its label above (revised 2026-10-22: nothing asked twice):
   - "IFSC": uppercase, with the helper "11 characters, e.g. ABCD0123456". Once valid, a read-only line under it shows the bank and branch: "State Bank of India · Bandra branch".
   - "Account number": masked input. (No "Confirm account number": the account check returns the holder name, which confirms it.)
   - "Bank proof": an upload of a cancelled cheque or statement.
3. A note: "For your security we'll send a code to your mobile before saving."
4. Primary button "Verify account".
5. A security dialog variant: "Confirm it's you", the line "Code sent to +91 XXXXXX3210", six digit boxes, and "Confirm".
6. Success variant: "Account verified", with a definition list ("Account": XXXXXX4321; "IFSC": ABCD0123456; "Account holder": SAMPLE TRADERS PVT LTD).
7. Name-mismatch info variant: "The account holder name differs from your business name. Our team will review this."
8. Failure variant: "We couldn't verify this account. 2 attempts left."
