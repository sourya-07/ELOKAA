Signatory KYC, manual option (step 7 of 13 on the step-by-step path; owner request 2026-10-22). The applicant can verify the signatory without DigiLocker by uploading ID documents, which Eko staff check by hand (STF-04).

1. Title "Verify the signatory". Two choice cards: "Verify with DigiLocker" (APP-09) and "Upload ID documents" (selected).
2. For "Upload ID documents":
   - "ID type" select: Aadhaar card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving licence.
   - Upload tiles "Front" and "Back" (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 10 MB each).
   - Helper for Aadhaar: "Cover the first 8 digits of the Aadhaar number before uploading."
   - Upload tile "Signatory PAN card".
   - Note: "Our team checks these by hand. We'll tell you if anything is unclear."
   - Black button "Submit for review".
3. Variants: tiles with the pill "Waiting for review"; a rejected tile with a reviewer note and "Replace".

No time estimates. Nothing asked twice: the signatory name comes from APP-07.
