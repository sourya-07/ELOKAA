The Eko staff review console: the sign-in page and the review queue of businesses applying to become ELOKA platform owners. The UI is dense, calm and built for desk work.

**PLATFORM:** Web, Desktop-first (1280px)

**FRAME A — Sign-in (STF-01):**
1. A centred card on the warm canvas: the "ELOKA" wordmark, the label "Staff console", and the title "Sign in".
2. A secondary-style button "Sign in with Google" with the Google "G" mark, and a helper line "Use your Eko work account".
3. A divider labelled "Development only", then a compact mobile + OTP form.
4. Error variants: "Your account isn't enabled for the staff console. Ask an admin." and "Google sign-in isn't configured on this environment."

**FRAME B — Review queue (STF-02):**
1. **Left sidebar** (240px): the "ELOKA Staff" wordmark; navigation items "Queue" (active) and "Assigned to me"; and at the bottom the signed-in user "Asha Sample · Reviewer" with "Sign out".
2. **Page header:** the title "Applications", with a subtitle showing the number of results, "42 applications".
3. **Filter bar:**
   - a multi-select "Status" (default: Submitted, Under review, On hold)
   - a toggle "Assigned to me"
   - a search field "Search company, application ID or PAN"
   - a "Clear filters" text button
4. **Table:**
   - Columns: Application (monospace ID like onb_7Q2K9X), Company, Entity type, Status (pill), Submitted (date), Assigned to, Flags (small warning count badge).
   - Six fictional rows with mixed statuses ("Submitted", "Under review", "On hold", "Correction required").
   - Rows have a hover highlight and are clickable.
5. **Footer:** a "Load more" secondary button.
6. **States:**
   - Empty: "No applications match these filters", with a "Clear filters" button.
   - Loading: 6 skeleton rows.
