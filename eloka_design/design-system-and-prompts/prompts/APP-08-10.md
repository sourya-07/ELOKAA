Three onboarding steps that use the standard step layout (top bar, "Step N of 13", step title, form card, sticky primary button): Email verification (step 6), Signatory KYC with DigiLocker (step 7) and Live photo (step 8).

**PLATFORM:** Web, Mobile-first

**FRAME A — Email verification:**
1. Title "Business email".
2. A labelled field "Email address", then the button "Send code".
3. Second state: "Enter the 6-digit code sent to s••••@sampletraders.in" with a "Change email" link, six digit boxes, a "Resend code in 0:45" line, and the button "Verify email".
4. Success state: a success line "Email verified" with a check icon.

**FRAME B — Signatory KYC:**
1. Title "Verify the signatory with DigiLocker".
2. A short explanation card, "DigiLocker will share the signatory's name and the last 4 digits of their Aadhaar with ELOKA. We never see or store the full Aadhaar number." It has three small bullet steps: "Sign in to DigiLocker", "Allow access", "You'll come back here automatically".
3. Primary button "Continue to DigiLocker", with an external-link icon.
4. Pending variant: "Waiting for DigiLocker…", a small progress indicator, and a "Check again" link.
5. Success variant: a definition list ("Name": RAVI SAMPLE; "Aadhaar": XXXX-XXXX-1234) and then "Continue".
6. Failed variant: "We couldn't complete the DigiLocker check. 2 attempts left.", with a "Try again" button.

**FRAME C — Live photo:**
1. Title "Live photo of the signatory".
2. A camera viewport with an oval face guide, and a hint line "Face the camera in good light. Remove glasses."
3. A capture button. Then a preview state with the buttons "Retake" and "Use photo".
4. An optional checkbox "Share my location with this photo".
5. Permission-denied variant: a card "Camera access is blocked" with short steps to allow it in the browser settings, and a "Use another device" link.
