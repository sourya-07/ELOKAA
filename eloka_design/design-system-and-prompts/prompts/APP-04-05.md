Two consecutive onboarding steps in one frame set: Consent (step 2 of 13) and Program selection (step 3 of 13). Each step uses the standard step layout.

**PLATFORM:** Web, Mobile-first

**STEP LAYOUT (shared):**
- Top bar with the "ELOKA" wordmark and "Help".
- Progress line "Step N of 13" and a back link "Application".
- Step title, with a small "Saved" indicator next to it.
- A form card.
- A sticky full-width primary button at the bottom.

**FRAME A — Consent:**
1. Title "Consent and terms".
2. A scrollable text panel (about 12 lines visible) with the section headings "How we use your information" and "Terms of service", plus a small version label "Version 2026-10-01".
3. A list of the specific things the applicant agrees to, each as a short line with a check icon: "Verify your business and identity details with official sources", "Store your documents securely for compliance", "Contact you about this application".
4. A checkbox "I have read and agree to the terms and the consent above".
5. Primary button "Agree and continue". It is disabled until the checkbox is ticked.
6. Variant notice: "The terms have been updated. Please read and accept the latest version."

**FRAME B — Program:**
1. Title "Your business type".
2. An info row: "Program: White-label platform (Enterprise)".
3. Four large selectable radio cards, each with a one-line description:
   - Private Limited Company
   - LLP
   - Partnership firm
   - Sole proprietorship
4. A help line: "This decides which documents we ask for."
5. A confirmation dialog variant: "Changing your business type will reset your PAN, documents and agreement steps", with buttons Cancel and Change.
6. Primary button "Continue".
