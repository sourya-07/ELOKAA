White-label setup (step 11 of 13). The applicant names their platform, picks its web address, uploads a logo and chooses brand colours, with a live preview of their future app.

**PLATFORM:** Web. Mobile shows the preview below the form; desktop shows the form on the left and a sticky preview on the right.

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Header:** the standard step layout with the title "Set up your platform".
2. **App name:** a labelled field "Platform name", helper text "Shown to your agents and customers. Up to 60 characters."
3. **Web address:**
   - A labelled field "Web address" with a suffix label ".eloka.app".
   - Live availability states under the field: checking (small spinner, "Checking…"), available (check icon, "sampletraders.eloka.app is available"), taken ("This address is taken. Try one of these:" with suggestion chips "sampletradersin", "sample-traders", "stpay").
   - Format error: "Use 3–40 lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens".
4. **Logo:** an optional upload area "Upload logo (PNG or JPG, square works best)" with a preview thumbnail and a "Remove" link.
5. **Brand colours:**
   - A row of six ready-made palette swatches (each showing primary and accent).
   - A "Custom" option that reveals primary and accent colour pickers.
   - A "Navigation style" segmented control: "Dark" / "Light".
   - A contrast warning variant: "Text on this colour may be hard to read. We'll use a darker shade for buttons."
6. **Live preview:** a framed miniature of the future app showing a top bar with the logo and platform name, a side menu with three items, and a sample card with a primary button, all in the chosen colours. Caption: "Preview".
7. **Action:** sticky primary button "Save and continue".
