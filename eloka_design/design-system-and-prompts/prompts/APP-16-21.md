The after-submission and system screens of the applicant flow: status, corrections, final outcomes, and the shared dialogs and error states.

**PLATFORM:** Web, Mobile-first

**FRAME A — Submitted / under review (APP-16):**
1. Title "Application submitted", with a status pill "Under review".
2. A "What happens next" card with three short lines: "Our team checks your details and documents", "We may ask you to change something", "We'll email and SMS you with the decision".
3. "Submitted 21 Oct 2026, 10:42".
4. A timeline card "Activity" with four events, each with a time and a status pill: "Application submitted", "Reviewer assigned", "Document verified", "Under review".
5. A ghost button "Withdraw application" (shown only before review starts).
6. Empty-timeline variant: "Nothing yet. We'll update this page as your application moves."

**FRAME B — Corrections required (APP-17):**
1. A warning banner "Our team asked for 2 changes", with the reviewer's message and "Please fix by 5 Nov 2026 (6 days left)".
2. "Needs changes" list:
   - "Documents — Certificate of incorporation: the stamp is not readable" with a "Fix" button.
   - "Bank account — please upload a recent statement" with a "Fix" button.
3. "Also needs a quick re-check": "Declaration", "Agreement".
4. Primary button "Resubmit application", disabled until the items are fixed.

- Approved: "Approved. We're setting up your platform at sampletraders.eloka.app. We'll contact you when it's ready." Action: "Contact support".
- Rejected: "We couldn't approve your application", the reason message from the reviewer, and "Contact support".
- Withdrawn: "You withdrew this application", and "Start a new application".
- Expired: "This application expired after 30 days without activity", and "Start again".
